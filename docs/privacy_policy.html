Focus Forest Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Focus Forest stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

Your focus sessions, statistics, and app settings remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not store payment information.

The app may fetch remote configuration from our server to enable or disable Google Play billing. No personal data is sent in this request.

Contact: support@focusforest.app
